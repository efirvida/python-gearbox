## [0.2.0] - unreleased

### Added
* Double helical gears (`Gear.double_helical`) in ISO 6336 (KI-38).
* Internal gears (`Gear.internal`) in ISO 6336-1/2/3 and in the AGMA 2001/908 pitting calculation (KI-39).
* ISO 6336-1 mesh load factor, methods A and C of the dynamic factor, static strength, limited pitting,
  grinding notches (`Y_Sg`), shaper-cutter root geometry; AGMA 2001-D04 allowable stresses and safety
  factors; ISO 1328-1:2013 flank tolerances.
* Contact ratio limited by the base circle, ISO 6336-2 formula (33) (KI-40).
* `gearbox.fem` (optional, `pip install python-gearbox[fem]`):
  * structured quad/hex Gmsh meshes of gear sectors and gear pairs (`mesh_gear`, `mesh_pair`;
    2D/3D, linear or quadratic elements, surface grading, `fillet_block` (default) and `standard`
    block layouts, any mesh position of the pair with `pinion_rotation`) with named node and
    element sets;
  * tooth bending (`solve_bending`) and tooth contact of a pair (`solve_contact`, frictionless
    penalty contact under the nominal torque) with the external CalculiX solver. In 2D the root
    stress is checked against ISO 6336-3 method B (within 2-12 %) and the contact pressure at the
    pitch point against Hertz (within 3 %);
  * `gearbox.fem.post`: line of action, contact sweeps along the path of contact, contact per
    tooth and matplotlib plots of meshes, stress fields and the line of action.

### Changed
* Complete refactor: Python >= 3.11, immutable typed dataclasses, descriptive names, test suite.
* ISO 6336 updated from the 2006 to the **2019** edition. **Numbers differ from 0.1.x**; see
  [KNOWN_ISSUES.md](KNOWN_ISSUES.md) (KI-01 to KI-37) for every change and its effect.
* AGMA geometry factors `I` and `J` verified against the AGMA 908-B89 tables.
* The tooth form of the exporters is generated by the rack (tool tip radius `ρ_fP`), also for
  helical gears, instead of a circular root fillet; the exported fillet coordinates change (KI-41).
* ISO 6336-1 `K_v` method B: the running-in tip relief `C_ay` used `1/8` instead of `1/18`
  (table 8); only gears of tolerance class 5 or better change, slightly (KI-42).
